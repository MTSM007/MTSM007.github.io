Questions

What does the deposit cover?

The deposit is $2,500 to $5,000, depending on the role. It covers the work to launch your search, is credited in full toward the 20% fee, and is non-refundable. If the search is paused, it stays credited toward a hire for 6 months.

When is the rest of the fee due?

When your hire starts. If they accept but never start, nothing more is due and I keep searching.

How does the refund guarantee work?

If your hire resigns or is let go for performance within 90 days (180 for Director-level and above), you get part of the fee back. The sooner they leave, the more you get back.

What if the role changes, or we put the search on hold?

Tell me and the search pauses at no extra charge. Your deposit stays credited toward a hire for 6 months, including if we restart with an updated role.

How does the embedded retainer work?

I work as a recruiter inside your team, 40 hours a week on up to 4 roles at once, for $12,000 a month. Two-month minimum, then month to month.

Full terms are set out in our client agreement.
