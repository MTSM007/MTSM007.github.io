The five-part kickoff

The same structure I use to start every search.

  1. 1
    RoleWho the person reports to, who they work with, and what they’re responsible for.
  2. 2
    ContextWhy the role is open now, its history, and how the search will run.
  3. 3
    Success profileWhat success looks like, the skills it takes, and where to find it.
  4. 4
    Compensation and logisticsThe package, and where and how the person will work.
  5. 5
    ProcessHow candidates will be interviewed and chosen, and how you’ll work together.
