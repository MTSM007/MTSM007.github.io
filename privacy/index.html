Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

Morley Talent Solutions Inc. handles personal information in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Michael Morley is responsible for privacy. For questions, complaints, or to access or correct your information, email michael@hire.morleytalentsolutions.com.

What I collect

Contact details and hiring needs from the Start hiring form; name, email, location and role interests from job-alert sign-ups; and candidate information you provide or have made public for professional purposes, such as a LinkedIn profile.

How I use it

To respond to enquiries, provide recruiting services and, with your consent, send job alerts. You can unsubscribe or withdraw consent at any time.

Sharing

Candidate information is shared with prospective employers with the candidate's consent. I use service providers for email, website hosting and spam protection, which may store information outside Canada, where it is subject to local laws.

Retention

Information is kept only as long as needed for these purposes.

Cookies

The forms use spam protection that may set cookies. This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
